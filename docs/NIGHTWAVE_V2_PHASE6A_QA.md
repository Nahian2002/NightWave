# NightWave Phase 6A — Cinematic UI, Design Consistency and Responsive QA

**Branch:** `feature/nightwave-v2-phase6a-cinematic-ui-system` (stacked on Phase 5Z). **Draft source.** Android compilation, screenshot captures, installed APK, cross-device cloud verification and real-world playback **deliberately deferred**. Preserve stable `main` and existing phone app data.

## Design principles implemented
1. **Editorial hierarchy, not copied Spotify screens.** Home now begins with a contextual daypart greeting, the existing local guest display name, and real counts for liked songs and recently played tracks. No simulated charts, play counts, AI covers or playlists.
2. **Meaningful visual identity.** `NightWaveVisualLanguage` selects a stable accent from a track/collection identifier and blends it into a GPU-light radial Canvas atmosphere. Mood tiles have layered abstract rings, not fake media artwork; art in the hero, lists and player still comes from actual saved thumbnails with a verified fallback.
3. **Cohesive Home.** Genuine Phase 5Y recommendation mixes get an editorial card hierarchy and remain preview-before-play. Six mood shortcuts receive their own balanced artwork treatments; tapping them still follows the existing search navigation.
4. **Search-first discovery.** Search input precedes presets and uses a clear action. The official YouTube browser is still the main route; optional native quick results remain separate. Saved local search never depends on providers and the existing stale-query guard remains.
5. **Library ownership remains unchanged.** Compact counts for liked music and saved playlists, better collection cards, clearable playlist filter and UI-only sort chips: Saved order / Title / Artist. Sorting does **not** rewrite playlists; the queue follows the selected sorted/filtered collection when a track is tapped.
6. **Compact adaptive navigation.** Active tab displays the icon and text in a rounded capsule; other tabs keep discoverable icon content descriptions. Original test tags, semantic selection flags, pager gestures, Back button routing, insets and reduced-motion transitions remain.
7. **Immersive Now Playing.** A soft static, identity-keyed gradient behind actual artwork, subtle cover border, clearer local/public source indicator, larger accessible play control and refined queue affordance. The mini-player has a related accent gradient and 3dp progress bar. Media3 service remains the only playback owner.
8. **Premium You page.** Consistent heading, neutral profile card and a truthful local/backup status; the 5U/5Z cloud controls and all existing import, export and rollback actions remain where they were.
9. **No new permissions, ads, paid assets, analytics, fonts, remote thumbnails, database migrations, logging of secrets or stream extraction changes.** The theme/reduced-motion settings are still respected; decorative visuals do not animate continuously.

## Later verification checklist (NOT executed now)
1. Build with `build-windows.cmd` and require JVM tests + `:app:assembleDebug` + `:app:compileDebugAndroidTestKotlin`. Record compiler output and check APK version/signature against the installed NightWave before an in-place update. Never uninstall or clear data to fix signing.
2. Inspect Home, Search, Library, You and Now Playing at 320dp, 360dp, 412dp, 600dp tablet, landscape/tablet, display font scale 1.0/1.3/1.8, Android light/dark and AMOLED themes. Check no cropped buttons, truncated vital state, sideways overflow, or obscured controls behind the mini-player.
3. Verify screen readers can reach Search clear action, profile Settings, tab icons (Home/Search/Library/You), track options, local Library sort chips, saved audio and queue. Confirm minimum ~48dp hit regions and contrast on all themes.
4. Verify **reduced motion** eliminates decorative navigation timing and full-player animation, and standard settings retain gesture responsiveness without jank. Check repeated Home/Library/Search tab swipes, Android Back, Up Next swipe/Undo, artwork gestures, mini-player drag, seek and queue management.
5. Enter an artist in Search; check saved matches work offline, official YouTube discovery opens, quick provider errors remain actionable, keyboard Search works and clear removes typed query without touching saved tracks.
6. Populate at least two playlists; change list contents while a sort/filter is selected. Ensure sorting is visual-only and does not modify stored playlist order. Tap a track and inspect the expected sorted filtered queue. Confirm empty playlists and collections are readable.
7. With a real downloaded thumbnail and with network disconnected, check Now Playing card background, art fallback, buffering state, active/paused/ended tracks, and Home spotlight Play again. No background download or new stream request should arise merely from opening the player.
8. Confirm Google Drive 5U backup and 5Z manual merge controls remain present, but do not connect a personal Google account until OAuth and account pinning are separately verified. Avoid any destructive restore on the primary phone.
9. Validate Home recommendations show only real saved music; no fabricated album covers, track titles, or duplicate playlist entries. Collect screenshots and an annotated layer/seam review across tabs before release.

## Constraints and next step
This is a **substantial source-level visual redesign**, not a measured UI/UX benchmark or approved release. Build/runtime/layout QA, Google sign-in and APK signing stay unverified. After this review the next incremental upgrade could be Phase 6B (supported lyrics, private listening insights, and queue-aware discovery) or 6A2 (device-captured visual QA refinements), according to actual phone screenshots and performance.
